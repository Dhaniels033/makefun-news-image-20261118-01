A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image — Trump in Manila and Shenzhen as APEC opens, the Crimea bridge collapse, fresh Iranian drone attacks over the Gulf, the Dow's fall and Nvidia's earnings, Anthropic's IPO pricing, and Cyclone Akvan.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) `news-image-20261118` competition. `main.ts` (Node standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory; the image was rendered from `frontpage.html` with headless Chromium at exactly 1024×1024.
