Asia · APEC opens today

Trump meets Marcos in Manila, then flies to Shenzhen as APEC opens

The US president holds talks with Ferdinand Marcos Jr. at Malacañang today before flying to China, where Vladimir Putin has already arrived. On Thursday, Trump, Xi Jinping and Putin meet three-way — the first such summit — with nuclear arms, Ukraine and Iran on the agenda. Xi opens the 33rd APEC leaders’ meeting, of 21 economies, in Shenzhen.

Europe · The war in Ukraine

Crimea bridge’s road arch collapses after second strike in two days

Kyiv says Neptune missiles and Palianytsia drones destroyed the 19-km Kerch bridge’s road span; traffic is suspended and two people were killed. Russia hit Kyiv’s North and South bridges and says it downed 1,441 drones.

The Gulf · Iran–US

Bahrain and Kuwait intercept fresh Iranian drone attacks

Bahrain’s defences repelled more Iranian attacks on Wednesday morning, a day after the Revolutionary Guards targeted a US radar at Sheikh Isa Air Base. US jets struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island; Washington says no US personnel were hurt.

Markets · Wall Street

Dow falls 850 points as Nvidia beats — and shuts the door on Huang

The Dow lost 1.64% and the S&P 500 fell 1.03% to its lowest close since July. After the bell, Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8bn, up 103%, and forecast $130bn — but its board said it will not reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive.

Technology · AI

Anthropic prices its giant IPO today; trading as ANT starts Thursday

The Claude maker is pricing 825m shares at $70–$78, raising up to $64.4bn at a valuation near $1.4 trillion. Demand is reported at about seven times the shares on offer; a $26bn private placement runs alongside.

IN BRIEF

LONDON: Britain sanctions eight Israeli firms building West Bank settlements, plus the Tzav 9 group accused of blocking Gaza aid. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband: “Do not bid, do not build.” Israel vowed countermeasures.
